# Course schedule

**MGMC01 – Metagenomic Data Analysis of Microbial Communities**

The course runs for **5 days** (Monday 26<sup>th</sup> to Friday 30<sup>th</sup> October 2026), with classes from **15:00 to 20:30** (Switzerland local time, GMT+2) each day. Every day is composed of two 2.5-hour sessions separated by a 30-minute break. Each session combines lecture and hands-on exercises, with dedicated time for discussing participants' own data, time permitting. All live sessions are recorded and made available the same day.

## Day 1 – Monday 26<sup>th</sup> (15:00–20:30)

!!! note "Time zone"
    All times are given in Switzerland local time (GMT+2). Sessions are recorded and made available on the same day to ensure accessibility for attendees across different time zones.

| Time      | Type       | Topic |
|-----------|------------|-------|
| 15:00–17:30 | Session 1 | **Introduction to Metagenomics, goals and opportunities**<br>Overview of metagenomics concepts and history; culture-independent approaches, study design considerations, and applications across environmental, clinical, and biotechnological fields; introduction to sequencing technologies, short and long reads. |
| 17:30–18:00 | Break     | |
| 18:00–20:30 | Session 2 | **Quality Control and Contamination Assessment**<br>Quality assessment of sequencing data; adapter removal, filtering strategies, contamination detection, and best practices for genomic and transcriptomic analyses. |

## Day 2 – Tuesday 27<sup>th</sup> (15:00–20:30)

| Time      | Type       | Topic |
|-----------|------------|-------|
| 15:00–17:30 | Session 3 | **Targeted sequencing I (Introduction and theory)**<br>Principles of amplicon-based sequencing; marker gene selection (16S, ITS), primer design, PCR amplification biases, and library preparation strategies for targeted metagenomics; OTUs vs ASVs. |
| 17:30–18:00 | Break     | |
| 18:00–20:30 | Session 4 | **Targeted sequencing II (Downstream analyses)**<br>Processing of amplicon sequencing data; OTU/ASV inference, taxonomic assignment, diversity metrics, and statistical approaches for microbial community comparison. |

## Day 3 – Wednesday 28<sup>th</sup> (15:00–20:30)

| Time      | Type       | Topic |
|-----------|------------|-------|
| 15:00–17:30 | Session 5 | **Whole genome sequencing and taxonomic classification**<br>Principles of shotgun metagenomic sequencing; library preparation, sequencing depth and coverage considerations, and read-based taxonomic classification using k-mer and alignment-based approaches. |
| 17:30–18:00 | Break     | |
| 18:00–20:30 | Session 6 | **Taxonomic classification and downstream analysis**<br>Taxonomic classification vs profiling, tools, diversity analysis, differential abundance testing, and result visualization strategies. |

## Day 4 – Thursday 29<sup>th</sup> (15:00–20:30)

| Time      | Type       | Topic |
|-----------|------------|-------|
| 15:00–17:30 | Session 7 | **Metagenomics assembly**<br>Principles of _de novo_ metagenomic assembly; assembly algorithms, coverage and community complexity challenges, quality metrics, and tools for short- and long-read data. |
| 17:30–18:00 | Break     | |
| 18:00–20:30 | Session 8 | **Draft genomes (bins) and Metagenome-Assembled Genomes (MAGs)**<br>Binning strategies for recovering individual genomes from assemblies; coverage- and composition-based approaches, and generation of draft genomes and MAGs. |

## Day 5 – Friday 30<sup>th</sup> (15:00–20:30)

| Time      | Type       | Topic |
|-----------|------------|-------|
| 15:00–17:30 | Session 9 | **MAG quality assessment, annotation and classification**<br>Evaluation of MAG completeness and contamination; genome annotation, taxonomic classification, and adherence to quality standards (e.g., MIMAG) for reporting draft genomes. |
| 17:30–18:00 | Break     | |
| 18:00–20:30 | Session 10 | **FAIR data and reproducibility**<br>Application of FAIR principles to metagenomic datasets; public repository deposition, metadata standards, workflow managers, and containerization for reproducible analyses. |

# Course schedule

**MGMC01 – Metagenomic Data Analysis of Microbial Communities**

The course runs for **5 days** (Monday 26<sup>th</sup> to Friday 30<sup>th</sup> October 2026), with classes from **14:00 to 19:30** (UK local time, GMT) each day. Every day is composed of two 2.5-hour sessions separated by a 30-minute break. Each session combines lecture and hands-on exercises, with dedicated time for discussing participants' own data, time permitting. All live sessions are recorded and made available the same day.

## Day 1 – Monday 26<sup>th</sup> (14:00–19:30)

    All times are given in UK local time (GMT). Sessions are recorded and made available on the same day to ensure accessibility for attendees across different time zones.

| Time      | Type       | Topic |
|-----------|------------|-------|
| 14:00–16:30 | Session 1 | **Introduction to Metagenomics, goals and opportunities**<br>Overview of metagenomics concepts and history; culture-independent approaches, study design considerations, and applications across environmental, clinical, and biotechnological fields; introduction to sequencing technologies, short and long reads. |
| 16:30–17:00 | Break     | |
| 17:00–19:30 | Session 2 | **Quality Control and Contamination Assessment**<br>Quality assessment of sequencing data; adapter removal, filtering strategies, contamination detection, and best practices for genomic and transcriptomic analyses. |

| Time      | Type       | Topic |
|-----------|------------|-------|
| 14:00–16:30 | Session 3 | **Targeted sequencing I (Introduction and theory)**<br>Principles of amplicon-based sequencing; marker gene selection (16S, ITS), primer design, PCR amplification biases, and library preparation strategies for targeted metagenomics; OTUs vs ASVs. |
| 16:30–17:00 | Break     | |
| 17:00–19:30 | Session 4 | **Targeted sequencing II (Downstream analyses)**<br>Processing of amplicon sequencing data; OTU/ASV inference, taxonomic assignment, diversity metrics, and statistical approaches for microbial community comparison. |
